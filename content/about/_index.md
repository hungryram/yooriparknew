Yoori Park is a distinguished Estate Agent at Hilton & Hyland | Forbes Global Properties, known for her extensive expertise in the luxury property market. With a keen eye for design and a deep understanding of high-end real estate trends, Yoori has successfully represented sellers and buyers in some of the most coveted neighborhoods. Her global perspective, fluency in multiple languages, and in-depth knowledge of both local and international markets make her uniquely equipped to serve a diverse clientele.

Yoori’s experience spans residential sales, luxury rentals, developments, and investment properties, where her strategic negotiation skills and market insight have consistently delivered exceptional results. With a firsthand, thorough comprehension of Los Angeles’ permitting and regulatory processes and extensive sales experience, Yoori has built a reputation for providing personalized service, ensuring her clients achieve their real estate goals and secure favorable outcomes.

Beyond her real estate expertise, Yoori brings a strong background in design, helping clients envision the full potential of a property and offering valuable guidance on enhancing its appeal. With a Bachelor’s degree in Communications from the University of Washington, her passion for architecture and interior aesthetics adds an extra dimension to her client services, enabling her to provide a truly holistic approach to real estate.

Yoori continues to deliver outstanding results, driven by her commitment to excellence, professionalism, and unwavering dedication to her clients.
